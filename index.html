FAQ

Common questions people ask before they book

A real website should answer the obvious stuff fast and still make it easy to reach a person.

How does the estimate request work?

You enter the address, choose the services you need, answer a few quick questions, and get a ballpark range. Final pricing can still be confirmed after review.

Can I still just call or text?

Yes. The site keeps call, text, and Facebook contact options visible so customers can use whatever feels easiest.

Can exterior windows be included?

Yes. Exterior window cleaning is built in as a separate service option so it can be added to house-washing jobs or requested by itself.

Does the online estimate lock in the final price?

No. It is meant to give a reasonable starting range and collect better job details before follow-up.
